Mozart composes heterogeneous accelerators in a shared-memory SoC through a modular accelerator synchronization interface (ASI) with 1% area overhead that reduces control tax. We chained 15 pipelined accelerators running real applications on Linux across 70+ FPGA-prototyped configurations.
